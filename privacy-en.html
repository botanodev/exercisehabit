Exercise Habit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Last updated: October 15, 2026

Summary

This app has no accounts, uses no servers, and does not connect to the internet. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. The developer cannot access it.

Information you enter

This includes your habit settings (identity statement, cue, weekly count, reminder times), your daily check-ins and optional notes, your questionnaire answers, and app settings. All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.

The app has no sign-in and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this information; it cannot be recovered.

Network

The app has no internet permission and does not connect to the network. Exercise demonstration videos and images are bundled inside the app.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device and do not go through any server. When you tap a button on a notification, the record is written directly to the data on your device.

Android backup

If you have Google backup turned on, Android may back up this app's records and settings to your Google account. This is governed by Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder and the bedtime reminder.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration: for side-switch cues in the guided timer and for reminder notifications.
  • Keep awake: to keep the screen on during the guided timer, and to handle notification buttons in the background.
  • Network state: declared by the built-in video player component. The app has no internet permission and cannot connect.

The app requests no other permissions.

Purchases and ads

This version has no paid features and shows no ads. If they are added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts; no collection of names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No uploading of any records.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No location data; no step count or other sensor data.
  • No selling or sharing of user data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. The developer keeps no user data.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted at this same address, with the "Last updated" date revised.
