Exercise Habit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Last updated: October 30, 2026

Summary

This app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, and the developer cannot access it. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, and the full version is bought through Google Play. Neither of them receives your habits, check-ins or notes.

Information you enter

This includes your habit settings (identity statement, cue, weekly count, reminder times), your daily check-ins and optional notes, your questionnaire answers, and app settings. All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.

The app has no sign-in and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this information; it cannot be recovered.

Network

The app connects to the internet only for the following. Your records are never sent.

  • Loading ads in the free version (see "Ads"). This stops once you buy the full version.
  • Ad consent: before any ad is loaded, the app asks Google whether users in your region must be asked for advertising consent first (the EU and the UK require this). The answer is based on your IP address; the app never reads your location. A consent form appears only where one is required.
  • Checking your purchase with Google Play (see "Purchases").

Exercise demonstration videos and images are bundled inside the app.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device and do not go through any server. When you tap a button on a notification, the record is written directly to the data on your device.

Android backup

If you have Google backup turned on, Android may back up this app's records and settings to your Google account. This is governed by Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Users who have not bought the full version see banner ads, served by Google AdMob.

To deliver ads, Google accesses information on your device, which may include:

  • Advertising ID
  • Basic device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate region)
  • Ad impressions and clicks

This data is collected and used by Google and is not passed to the developer. The developer sees only aggregated revenue statistics, which cannot be traced to any individual user. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can limit ad tracking: Android's Settings → Google → Ads lets you delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation. You will still see ads, just less relevant ones.

Buying the full version removes all ads.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase, completed entirely on Google Play's own screens. You never enter card details in this app, and the developer never sees your card number, billing address or any payment details. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

To know whether you have bought the full version, the app asks Google Play each time it starts and remembers the answer on your device, so the full version keeps working offline. No other purchase service is used. Because the purchase is tied to your Google account, it can be restored after you reinstall or change phones.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Once a refund completes, the full version is removed the next time the app checks with Google Play.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the daily reminder and the bedtime reminder.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration: for segment-change cues in the guided timer and for reminder notifications.
  • Keep awake: to keep the screen on during the guided timer; to keep segment cues on time during the outdoor timer while the screen is off; and to handle notification buttons in the background.
  • Foreground service (health type): for the outdoor timer (walking and walk to run). It keeps timing when you leave the app or turn off the screen, and shows the current segment and time left in the notification bar. It stops when the timer ends.
  • High sampling rate sensors: Android requires health-type foreground services to declare one of a set of permissions, and this is one of them. The app does not read any sensor and does not record steps, heart rate or location.
  • Internet and network state: for the connections listed under "Network".
  • Advertising ID: lets Google AdMob use the advertising ID to deliver ads (see "Ads").
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore the full version.

The app requests no other permissions.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts; no collection of names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No uploading of your habits, check-ins or notes.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No location data; no step count or other sensor data.
  • No selling of data — the developer holds no user data that could be sold.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Retention and deletion

  • What you enter: stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data: retained and deleted by Google under its own policy; you can control it through the Android ad settings described above.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play under its own policy. This is what lets your purchase be restored after you reinstall.

The developer keeps no user data.
